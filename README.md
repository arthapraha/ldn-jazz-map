# London jazz maps (unofficial)

Unofficial, fan-made maps of London jazz festival events, published with GitHub Pages.

- **2026:** [Unofficial map of EFG London Jazz Festival 2026 events](https://arthapraha.github.io/ldn-jazz-map/2026/), with 350 events from 9 to 22 Nov 2026.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Serious Ltd, EFG or the London Jazz Festival. "London Jazz Festival" is a registered trade mark of its owner. Event facts (titles, dates, venues and times) were collected from the official festival site on 26 Oct 2026 and may have changed; always check the official event page before you go.

Photos are from Wikimedia Commons, each under the licence named in its credit line. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Venue locations come from postcodes.io (contains OS and Royal Mail data © Crown copyright and database right). Built with Leaflet.
